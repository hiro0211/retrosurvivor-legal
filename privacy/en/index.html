1Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how information is handled in the mobile game "RetroSurvivor" for iOS and Android (the "App"). The App is provided by an independent developer ("the developer," "we," "us").

The App has no account registration, and the developer does not operate any game server. We never ask you to enter your name, address, or phone number.

2Information We Handle

2-1. Information stored only on your device (never sent)

  • Game records (scores, progress, in-game coins and upgrades)
  • App settings (volume, language, etc.)

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

2-2. Information handled by Google to serve ads

The App shows banner ads, interstitial (full-screen) ads, and rewarded ads (ads you may choose to watch for an in-game reward) through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK for consent. To serve ads, the following information is sent from your device to Google and handled by Google:

  • Device identifiers such as advertising IDs (on iOS, the IDFA is used only if you allow tracking through App Tracking Transparency (ATT); on Android, the advertising ID and App Set ID)
  • IP address (which may be used to estimate your approximate location)
  • Device and app information (model, operating system and version, language, app name and version, etc.)
  • Ad interactions such as impressions, taps, and video views
  • Diagnostic information (crash logs and performance data about the SDK)
  • Your ad consent choices (also stored on your device)

Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies, such as identifiers and on-device storage, to serve ads. To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

2-3. Information you give us when you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address, your name (if included), and the content of your message to reply to you.

2-4. In-app purchases (if added in the future)

We may add an in-app purchase to remove ads. If we do, payments will be processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), and the developer will not receive your credit card or other payment details. We will update this Policy before adding it.

2-5. Information we do not collect

  • Your name, address, or phone number (unless you include them in a message to us)
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise (GPS) location
  • Data from analytics or crash-reporting tools (the App currently uses none; we will update this Policy if that changes)

3How Information Is Used

  • To let you play the game (saving your records and settings)
  • To show ads and deliver rewards for rewarded ads
  • To measure ads and prevent invalid clicks and impressions (performed by Google)
  • To respect the choices you make in the consent screens
  • To reply to your inquiries

5Ad Consent and How to Change or Withdraw It

The App uses Google's UMP SDK to show consent screens required by the laws of your region.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch, we ask for your consent to store cookies or similar data and to use information for advertising. You can play without consenting. In that case, Google shows non-personalized or limited ads.
  • iOS: we ask for permission to track through Apple's ATT prompt (an explanation screen may be shown first). If you do not allow tracking, the IDFA is not used. The App works the same either way.
  • Certain US states: you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for advertising.

How to change or withdraw your choices

  • Open "Privacy Settings" in the App's settings screen to show the consent screen again and make a new choice. This button appears only in regions where the law requires it.
  • On iOS, you can change tracking permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on many devices, Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Menu names vary by device.

Step-by-step instructions are on the Support page.

6Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

If you live in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or an equivalent law applies. We request the consent required by Google's EU User Consent Policy.

6-1. Legal bases for processing

  • Storing cookies or similar data and processing for personalized ads: consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a))
  • Serving non-personalized ads, ad measurement, and fraud prevention: legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), or consent where required
  • Replying to inquiries: legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f))

6-2. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, and port your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. To exercise your rights, please contact us. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Note that the developer does not hold advertising data; it is managed by Google. For data held by Google, you can also exercise your rights through the Google Privacy Policy.

7US State Privacy Laws (CCPA / CPRA, etc.)

This section applies to residents of California and other US states with privacy laws.

  • Categories of information: identifiers (advertising IDs, IP address), internet or other electronic activity (ad interactions), approximate location inferred from IP address, and device information. See Section 2 for details.
  • Using advertising IDs and similar data for Google's advertising may be considered "sharing" (for cross-context behavioral advertising) or a "sale" of personal information under state laws. We do not sell personal information for money.
  • How to opt out: in the App's settings screen, open "Privacy Settings" and choose "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information." Your choice is passed to Google, which then restricts how ads are served.
  • You have the right to know what information is handled, to request deletion or correction, and not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights. Please contact us.

8Children's Privacy (Age)

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or, in the EEA and similar regions, under the age of digital consent in their country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have unintentionally received information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

9Retention and Deletion

  • Game records and settings: stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes them all. Because we do not have them, we cannot restore or delete them for you.
  • Ad consent status: stored on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the App, and you will be asked again after reinstalling.
  • Advertising data handled by Google: retained under Google's policies. See How Google retains data. Resetting your advertising ID breaks the link to your previous ID.
  • Inquiry emails: kept only as long as needed to reply, then deleted. To request deletion, please contact us.

10Security

  • We do not operate a game server and do not collect your game records.
  • The Google Mobile Ads SDK sends data over encrypted connections (TLS).
  • Inquiry emails are kept in an email account that only the developer can access.

11International Processing

Because we use third-party services such as Google, information may be processed in data centers outside Japan, including in the United States. These companies use safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

12Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when laws change or when we add features (such as in-app purchases or analytics tools). When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date on this page. For significant changes, we will also notify you in the App.
