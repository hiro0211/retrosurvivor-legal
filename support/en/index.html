2How to Change Your Ad Consent

The App shows ads through Google AdMob and uses Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) to show the consent screens required in your region. You can change your choices at any time as follows.

2-1. Choose again in the App (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and certain US states)

  1. Open the App and go to the settings screen from the title screen.
  2. Tap "Privacy Settings."
  3. The consent screen appears again. Make your new choice and close it. The new choice takes effect right away.

If you live in certain US states, you can choose "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" on this screen.

The "Privacy Settings" button appears only in regions where the law requires a way to change consent. It is not shown in other regions, such as Japan.

2-2. Change tracking permission on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Go to Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  3. Turn the switch for "RetroSurvivor" on or off. When it is off, your IDFA (advertising identifier) is not used for ads.

If you turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track," no app will ask for tracking permission, and all requests are treated as denied.

2-3. Reset or delete your advertising ID on Android

  1. Open your device's Settings.
  2. Go to Google → Ads (on some devices, Privacy → Ads).
  3. Choose "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID."

Menu names and locations vary by device and Android version.

2-4. Start over

If you uninstall and reinstall the App, your saved consent choices are erased, and the consent screen appears again on the next launch. Note that all game records are erased too. Because records are stored only on your device, they cannot be recovered.

3FAQ

Can I transfer my progress to a new device?

No. The App has no accounts, and records are stored only on your device.

I watched a rewarded ad but did not get the reward.

If you close the ad before it ends, the reward is not delivered. A reward may also fail when your connection is unstable or no ad is available. If it keeps happening, please contact us with your device and app version.

Can I remove ads?

Not at the moment. We may add an in-app purchase to remove ads in the future.

I want to know how my information is handled, or request deletion.

Please see the Privacy Policy. You can send deletion and other requests to the email address above.
